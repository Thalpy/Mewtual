## Capture and closure

One private, non-Clone `MetadataWork` can retain the actual physical allocation
for finite count/Rename work after its facade closes. A fixed atomic slot limits
this to one active capsule. Capture uses only original cached negatives, a slot
CAS and infallible Arc clones. It allocates no observer or argument graph, takes
no account/cell/catalog lock and returns `None` without formatting an error.

Capture checks open-not-CLOSED, rather than Idle. An admitted uncertain operation
must be lendable again for exact retry. Slot availability therefore says nothing
about whether a new operation can start: the real cell and claim still decide.
Closing after capture leaves finite custody, not admission. Operations cannot
use that custody to create a new claim after close, expose warm success from a
closed claim or reopen JOIN.

The Current mount check now borrows its already-owned revocation atomic. The old
`cached_store()` created a new observer, including an Authority allocation and
watch subscription, despite being used at final cached edges. A real constructor
counter regression failed before this correction and passes afterward. Its
Result/error behavior stays unchanged. The new boolean is used directly for
fixed-refusal capture; it performs no error-string allocation either. Neither
form replaces actual file, row, membership or native-command checks.

## Retirement and memory

`MetadataWork` has FIRST its physical Arc and LAST a slot acknowledgement with
the original slot Arc and a narrow payment tail. It has no Drop body. Actual
last-Arc destruction therefore drains cell IO, buffers and Current before the
slot becomes available. The acknowledgement contains no Physical or cold-handler
back-reference. The fixed control invoice covers both Arc headers, the private
Option assembly wrapper, the sole capsule and transient retiring acknowledgement.
It grants no second maximum decode/preparation allocation.

The original normal3 cell keeps an admitted request and sealed candidate through
errors. Dropping a capsule does not clear that state or its poison. Another
capsule can recover the same request, while edited input refuses before new work.
Completed outgoing debt keeps its original claim active after capsule retirement;
new reads/writes cannot overlap those buffers. If the last physical owner drops
first, the debt's original finite session lease still excludes a real successor
opener until its files and buffers drain. Closing admission never shortens that
physical exclusion.

Retire the WHOLE work capsule and completed debt outside native/cell/catalog
guards. A final Current destructor can notify real observers. The future native
producer still has to implement and prove this caller-side ordering.

## Assembly and cold open

The physical Arc and slot are allocated while the original upgrade is wholly
armed, before Current extraction and the final uniqueness proof. Its private
`current: None` is assembly-only: capture, reads and writes all refuse it before
IO or claims. The destination's first closer is armed before the unique plain
move installs `Some(current)`. There is no exposed empty owner or weak loan.

The destination remains declared after its original retirement bundle. Both an
unwind before physical installation and one after destination construction close
first and retain actual session exclusion through old IO retirement. Strict cold
opening retains the same original inspection evidence through its common opener
and final exposure, as before. No persistence/wire grammar or native authority
is added by sharing physical custody.
